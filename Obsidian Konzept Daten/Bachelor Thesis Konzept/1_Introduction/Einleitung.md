While we may reference other works to introduce and motivate our research, the introduction remains succinct, typically spanning 1 to 3 pages in the case of theses.

A common introduction style involves a dedicated subsection or paragraph titled \enquote{Contribution} to explicitly outline the advancements and contributions made in this work.
Additionally, a brief section titled \enquote{Thesis Outline} is often included in longer texts like theses to provide a high-level overview of the chapter structure.

Ultimately, the research questions should be clearly formulated and easy to identify, typically presented as open, straightforward questions.
It is also encouraged to formulate hypotheses that align with your initial assumptions.
If not placed here, hypotheses are often found in the \enquote{Methodology} section instead.
